# A Room of One's Own

A room is not simply a place. It is a permission.

She had never had a room that was only hers. Every desk she used had belonged to someone else, every hour had been borrowed. When she finally closed the door, the silence felt less like absence than like a guest who had been waiting a long time.

If she had known how much that silence would matter, she would have asked for it sooner. But perhaps one cannot ask for a room until one has learned what to do inside it.
